# Mahroli Boutique & Desi Market — All-in-One E-Commerce + Tailoring PWA

A zero-subscription Progressive Web App (PWA) combining:
1. **Meesho-Style Online Storefront (`Shop Store`)**: Product cards with discount % badges, category filters (`Crochet & Handmade`, `Ladies Suits & Kurtis`, `Blouse & Dress Material`, `Local Daily Essentials`), size/variant selector, Add to Cart & Buy Now, pincode-based local delivery check (`332404`, `332402`, etc.), COD/UPI checkout, and WhatsApp order confirmation.
2. **Seller Product Manager (`Add / Manage Products`)**: Add new products with photos, MRP vs Selling Price, stock tracking (auto-deducts on order), and quick restock.
3. **Store Orders Dashboard (`Store Orders`)**: Track online customer orders (`New Order` -> `Packed` -> `Shipped` -> `Delivered`), print invoices, and send UPI payment links on WhatsApp.
4. **Ladies' Tailoring Dashboard (`+ Tailoring Order` & `Tailoring List`)**: Customer measurements, fabric/reference photos, stitching/advance/balance billing, UPI QR code, and WhatsApp alerts.

## Quick Setup
1. Upload all files to **Netlify Drop** or **Replit**.
2. Open the link in Chrome on Android -> tap **Install App** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open **UPI & Delivery** tab -> enter your **UPI ID**, **Seller WhatsApp Number** (e.g., `919876543210`), and **Allowed Pincodes** (or leave blank for All-India shipping).
